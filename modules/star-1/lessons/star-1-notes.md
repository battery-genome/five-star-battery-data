# Star 1 Lecture Notes

## What one-star battery data means

Star 1 is about making a battery dataset openly available on the web with a clear, permissive license so that other people can find it, cite it, and reuse it.

## Why this matters

- Publication is the first step from private lab output to reusable research infrastructure.
- A repository plus a persistent identifier makes the dataset citable.
- A permissive license removes ambiguity around reuse.

## What to focus on in this lesson

- Choose a repository workflow that supports metadata and public access.
- Capture the minimum metadata needed to describe the dataset and its creators.
- Pair the upload with a license that enables downstream reuse.
