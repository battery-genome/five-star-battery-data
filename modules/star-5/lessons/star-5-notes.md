# Star 5 Lecture Notes

## What five-star battery data means

Star 5 is about linking a battery dataset to external identifiers, ontologies, materials knowledge, and related research resources so it can participate in a wider knowledge graph.

## Why this matters

- Linked data supports richer queries than isolated files.
- External identifiers make it easier to connect materials, cells, tests, and datasets.
- This is the step that makes the course feel native to Battery Genome rather than just adjacent to it.

## What to focus on in this lesson

- Reuse identifiers from external knowledge sources where possible.
- Connect local battery metadata to broader graph structures.
- Treat the dataset as part of an ecosystem, not as a standalone file.
