# Student Discussion

This lesson captures reflections from students and practitioners applying Five-Star Battery Data in their own work.

## Discussion themes

- Which parts of the framework were most useful
- Where adoption became difficult in practice
- How open, structured, linked data changed day-to-day workflows
- What opportunities participants see for future battery-data infrastructure
