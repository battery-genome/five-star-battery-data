# Star 2 Lecture Notes

## What two-star battery data means

Star 2 is about turning raw or proprietary battery outputs into structured, machine-readable tables with consistent labels, units, and conventions.

## Why this matters

- Structured tables are easier to validate, search, plot, and reuse.
- Consistent column labels reduce ambiguity across tools and teams.
- This is the foundation for open formats, metadata, and linking in later lessons.

## What to focus on in this lesson

- Clean up raw cycler exports.
- Normalize timestamps, labels, and units.
- Organize the dataset so each row and column has a clear meaning.
