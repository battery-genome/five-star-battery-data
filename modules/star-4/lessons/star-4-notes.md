# Star 4 Lecture Notes

## What four-star battery data means

Star 4 is about describing a battery dataset with ontology-aligned metadata so that the meaning of the data is explicit to both people and machines.

## Why this matters

- Metadata turns raw files into interpretable research objects.
- Shared vocabularies make datasets interoperable across institutions and tools.
- JSON-LD and RDF-compatible structures prepare the data for linked workflows.

## What to focus on in this lesson

- Represent the battery, test, and dataset using controlled terms.
- Capture semantic meaning for fields and units.
- Use JSON-LD as a practical bridge between human-readable and machine-readable metadata.
